Status: **draft**. Brand is a placeholder `[Brand]` (Lightweight will not be used). Keyword choice is based on observed evidence (App Store autocomplete, competitor metadata, reviews); search volume / difficulty are **not yet validated** (AppTweak step pending).

## 1. Positioning for launch

**Lead audience:** people who already lift (or have just started) and want a fast gym log, often with a program sitting in Notes, a screenshot or another app.
**Second audience:** people who train better with a friend.
**Third:** beginners who need a plan (Build a plan).

**Why:** logging is the biggest search intent and the #1 praise in competitor reviews; no major tracker targets friends/accountability, import or Apple Watch in its first screenshots; Apple Watch problems are Strong's top complaint; people describe moving from Notes, paper and spreadsheets.

**Core promise:** a fast workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch: bring the program you already have, beat last time, and keep each other going with friends.

**Assessment of existing lines**
| Line | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "The workout tracker built for how you actually train." | Clear category, slightly generic. Fine in the description; too long for the first screenshot. |
| "Train with Gym Bros. Chase results — not numbers." | Do not use on the store page. "Gym Bros" reads as male-only to a stranger; "not numbers" contradicts a tracker whose job is numbers. |
| "A plan when you need one. Full control when you don't." | Clear and true. Good for the description / plan screenshot. |
| "Your gym bro. In dinosaur form." | Do not use: conflicts with the Gym Bro friends feature. |
