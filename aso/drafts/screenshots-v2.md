# Harda — App Store screenshots v2 (2026-10-20)

Based on: competitor first-three analysis (`research/screenshots.md`), US reviews (`research/reviews.md`), Apple rules (`research/apple-rules.md`), owner-confirmed features.

## Why this order
- In search results people see only the first 3 screenshots. They must answer: what is it, what do I get, why this one.
- Leaders (Hevy, Strong, StrengthLog, Dropset) all open with the logging screen + a short claim, then social proof by screen 2. We have no ratings yet and will not invent proof, so we use differentiators instead.
- None of the 9 leaders shows import/switching, friends/streaks or Apple Watch in its first three screenshots.
- Reviews: Apple Watch is Strong's #1 complaint and the most requested feature in Alpha/Dropset; Hevy is praised for friend accountability; people switch from Notes, paper and spreadsheets.
- Caption text may be indexed by Apple (unconfirmed), so headlines naturally include "workout tracker", "Apple Watch", "friends".

## Global rules
- Headline ≤ 7 words, max 2 lines, readable at thumbnail size; one supporting line max.
- 4+ content only (Guideline 2.3.8): no profanity, no "fucking"/"ass".
- No prices, "free", "free trial" (price reference).
- Seryozha is never called "gym bro" (Gym Bro = friends feature).
- Status bar 9:41, full battery, Wi-Fi + signal.
- US set in lb / in; EU sets in kg / cm.
- One demo dataset across every screen (below).
- Harda visual style from the launch carousel: dark background (#141513-ish) or light (#F2F3F0), bold rounded sans, orange accent #F26445, "Harda" wordmark top-left optional.
- Export 1320 × 2868 (and 1206 × 2622). Apple Watch: separate set, watch UI only.
