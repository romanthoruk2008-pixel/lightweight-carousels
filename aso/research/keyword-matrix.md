# Keyword ranking matrix (proxy) — US English, collected 2026-10-19

## Read this first: these are proxies
- **iTunes Search API** (`itunes.apple.com/search?entity=software&country=us&limit=25&term=…`) is **only an approximation** of real App Store search ranking. It does not personalise and may weight things differently from the iOS App Store app. It also gives **no** search volume, popularity or difficulty numbers, and none are reported here.
- **App Store web search** (`apps.apple.com/us/iphone/search?term=…`) was added as a second proxy. It returns about 11–12 results per term, in page order. On the generic terms where both proxies cover the leaders, the top 2–3 match. For example, "workout tracker" is Strong #1 and Hevy #2 in both.
- **Autocomplete** (`search.itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZSearchHints.woa/wa/hints?clientApplication=Software&term=…`, header `X-Apple-Store-Front: 143441-1,29` = US): **it works**. It returns up to 10 suggestions per prefix, as plain strings in a plist with **no priority or popularity values**. The suggestion order hints at what people type, but it is not a volume measure.
- Rate limits: the iTunes Search API returned intermittent HTTP 403 and was retried with backoff. Each term is a single snapshot taken at one moment.

Raw data:
- iTunes Search API: `raw/search/*.json`
- web search: `raw/websearch/*.html`
- autocomplete: `raw/hints/*.plist` and `raw/hints/hints.json`
- combined: `raw/keyword_matrix.json` and `raw/keyword_matrix.csv` (rank per competitor, per source)

## Key findings from the matrix
1. **Strong, Hevy and Fitbod dominate the core logging/tracker terms.**
   - Strong is #1 (API) on: workout tracker, gym log, workout log, gym tracker, log workout, workout logger, workout journal, weightlifting log, lift tracker, training log, strength tracker.
   - Hevy is #1–3 on most of the same terms, and #1 on "progressive overload", "lifting tracker" and "apple watch gym".
   - Fitbod leads the planner/plan/routine terms and "weight lifting".
   - RepCount, Setgraph and Gymverse fill positions 4–12.
2. **Programs/routines:** Fitbod, Gymverse, LADDER, Stronglifts and Boostcamp. "routine builder" and "routine planner" return **non-fitness** habit/daily-planner apps (no gym app in the top 10), so "routine" alone is not a gym query. "workout routine" is Fitbod/Gymverse.
3. **Progress-related terms:**
   - "progressive overload": Hevy #1, Strong #3, plus many small apps named "Progressive Overload".
   - "gym progress": Liftoff #1, Strong #2, Hevy #4.
   - "pr tracker": Strong #2.
   - "personal records", "body measurements", "body measurement tracker" and "progress photos" are owned by **small single-purpose apps**. No major tracker is in the top 10 of either proxy.
4. **Beginner terms are not owned by trackers.**
   - "beginner workout": Fitbod #2 (API); the rest are home-workout apps.
   - "gym for beginners" and "beginner gym workout plan": Gymverse #1.
   - "strength training for beginners" and "weight lifting for beginners": LazyFit, LADDER, small apps. None of Hevy, Strong or Fitbod is in the top 10.
5. **Friends/accountability terms are owned by small social apps**: GymRats, Challenges, Swolemates, Proof, GymBuddy*, Nudge, effit, Lifted, Forge…
   - Among trackers, only Fitbod (#5 "gym buddy"), JEFIT (#9 "gym buddy") and, on "gym streak", Gymverse #4, Hevy #6, Setgraph #7, Liftoff #8 and Strong #9 appear.
   - **No major tracker targets "workout with friends", "social workout" or "fitness accountability" in its name or subtitle.**
6. **Import/switch:** "import workout" and "workout import" return a 0-rating "Fitoor: AI Workout Import", "Health CSV Importer" and assorted home-workout apps. Boostcamp #9–10 and JEFIT #10 are the only trackers. **Practically unclaimed**, but autocomplete shows no demand signal for "import w…".
7. **Apple Watch:**
   - "apple watch workout" returns Apple Fitness, Activity tracker, Watch… with no lifting leader in the top 5 (SmartGym #6).
   - "apple watch gym": Hevy #1 (both proxies), Fitbod #4–6, JEFIT #4–6.
   - "lifting apple watch": SmartGym, Gravitus, Gymaholic, Setgraph, Gym Log+.
   - Only Gymaholic has "Apple Watch" in its subtitle.
8. **Brand "lightweight":**
   - API: Lightweight! Workout Analysis #1, Lightweight: Workout Tracker #3, LW: Gym Workout Tracker & Log #6. A text editor sits at #2.
   - Web: the two apps are #1–#2 and LW is #5.
   - "lightweight workout": the two apps are #1–#2 in both proxies.
   - "lightweight gym": API Gym Workout: Trainer & Tracker #1, LW #2, Workout Analysis #7; web: Workout Analysis #1, LW #2.
