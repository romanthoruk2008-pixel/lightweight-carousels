# First three App Store screenshots — competitors (US, iPhone)

Collected 2026-10-19. Source: the screenshot shelf on `apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>` (same images and order as `screenshotUrls` in the iTunes lookup). For each app, files 1–3 were downloaded at 600 px width to `shots/<app>/1.jpg … 3.jpg`, plus a side-by-side contact sheet `shots/<app>_sheet.jpg`. No app in this set showed a video preview in the server-rendered shelf. That was not verified on a device, so an app preview video could still play first on iOS.

Text is verbatim, with case kept as shown (ALL CAPS where the image uses caps). "Beginner test" is my judgement of whether someone who has never used a lifting tracker would understand what the app does from screenshot 1 alone.

## Cross-app patterns (observations)
1. **Screen 1 almost always shows the core logging/plan UI** (Hevy, Strong, StrengthLog, Fitbod, Dropset dashboard, HeavySet). Exceptions: Boostcamp (coach grid), Gymverse (lifestyle photo), Alpha (uncaptioned routine editor).
2. **Headline formulas**:
   - Verb + object: "LOG WORKOUTS", "ANALYZE YOUR WORKOUTS", "CUSTOMIZE YOUR EQUIPMENT".
   - Identity/positioning: "built for serious lifters", "Serious tracking for serious gym workouts".
   - Outcome: "GET STRONGER", "See how strong you really are", "Build muscle, build strength".
3. **Social proof in the first two screens** is common: Hevy (Apps We Love), Strong (3 million users), Fitbod (Editor's Choice + 274,000+ reviews), Alpha (App of the Day, 4.9 x 39,000), Gymverse (20M+ downloads), HeavySet (Lifewire award). Lightweight will not have this at launch.
4. **Progress/PR analytics appear by screen 3** for nearly all of them (Hevy, StrengthLog, Gymverse, HeavySet, Alpha auto-progression, Boostcamp progression).
5. **Dark UI dominates**: 7 of the 9 competitors use dark backgrounds (Strong and Hevy are the main light ones). Floating "pop-out" cards are used by Hevy, Fitbod and Gymverse.
6. **Not shown in the first 3 screenshots of any of the 9 competitors**: importing or switching from another app (only Boostcamp's "Ditch the spreadsheet" comes near), Apple Watch, friends/streaks/nudges (HeavySet mentions "share routines with friends" only in a caption), and AI import of a program from a photo or text. Anti-ads/free messaging appears only in StrengthLog ("Zero ads").
