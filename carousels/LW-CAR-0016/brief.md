| 1 | hook | CAN'T DO / A PULL-UP? |
| 2 | how it works | 6 Levels. In Order. / Hit the target on each level, then move up. / Dead Hang |
| 3 | level 1 | LEVEL 1 / Dead Hang / Hang from the bar with straight arms. Just hold on. |
| 4 | level 2 | LEVEL 2 / Scapular Pulls / Hang, then pull your shoulders down without bending your arms. |
