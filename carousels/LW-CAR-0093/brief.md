## Маркетингова логіка
- **«How to build» для спини.** Поради з рядком «do:».

## Пункти
- #1 **pull with your elbows**: your hands are just hooks. do: drive your elbows down and back.
- #2 **train width and thickness**: pulldowns build width. rows build thickness. do: one of each every back day.
- #3 **pause at the squeeze**: a short pause stops the swing. do: hold every row for one second.
- #4 **use straps when your grip fails**: your back should quit before your hands. do: straps for heavy rows and pulldowns.
- #5 **don’t skip rear delts**: they finish the look of your upper back. do: face pulls or reverse pec deck.
