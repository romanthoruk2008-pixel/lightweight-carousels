## Пункти
- #1 **train triceps as hard as biceps**: triceps are the bigger part of your upper arm. do: one pushdown and one overhead extension every arm day.
- #2 **curl in a stretch**: curls with your arms behind your body hit the biceps hard. do: incline dumbbell curls or bayesian cable curls.
- #3 **don’t skip hammer curls**: they thicken your arm from the side. do: hammer curls at the end of every pull day.
- #4 **slow down the way down**: swinging steals the work from your arms. do: take two seconds to lower every curl.
- #5 **train arms twice a week**: small muscles bounce back fast. do: add two arm sets to your push and pull days.
