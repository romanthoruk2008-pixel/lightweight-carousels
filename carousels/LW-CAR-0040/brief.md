# LW-CAR-0040 — «why you keep quitting the gym» (S2)

## Маркетингова логіка
- **Біль кожного:** майже всі хоч раз кидали зал. Людина впізнає свою причину й пише в коментарях. Підпис: «Which one got you last time? 👇».
- **Продукт природно:** причина #3 «не бачиш прогресу» вирішується записами, і фінал це закриває.

## Пункти
- #1 **you start too hard**: five days a week from zero burns you out fast. fix: start with 2-3 days a week.
- #2 **you have no plan**: you walk in and don’t know what to do. fix: follow one simple plan.
- #3 **you can’t see progress**: it feels like nothing changes. fix: write down every workout and look back each month.
- #4 **you wait for motivation**: motivation comes and goes. fix: pick fixed days and times, like a meeting.
- #5 **you compare yourself to others**: their year five against your week two. fix: compare yourself to last month only.
- #6 **one missed day turns into a month**: skipping once quietly becomes the habit. fix: never miss twice in a row.
- #7 **the goal feels too far away**: ‘look like that’ feels impossible. fix: aim for small wins, like one more rep.

**Фінал:** «harda tracks every workout, so you can see how far you’ve come.» + «log your workouts and look back at every one you’ve done.»
