## Пункти
- #1 **cable lateral raise**: constant tension on your side delts the whole rep. do: stand side-on and lead with your elbow.
- #2 **dumbbell lateral raise**: the classic width builder. do: light weight, stop at shoulder height, no swinging.
- #3 **wide-grip pull-ups**: they build the lats that spread your back. do: pull your elbows down to your sides.
- #4 **single-arm cable pulldown**: each lat works on its own through a big range. do: drive your elbow down to your hip.
- #5 **reverse pec deck**: rear delts round out your shoulders from the side. do: pull out wide, not back.
