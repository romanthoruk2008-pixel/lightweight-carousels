## Пункти
- #1 **prioritise side delts**: they are what makes you look wide. do: lateral raises on most upper days.
- #2 **press overhead**: a heavy press builds the whole shoulder. do: seated dumbbell or machine press once a week.
- #3 **train the back of the shoulder**: rear delts are the part most people forget. do: reverse pec deck or face pulls.
- #4 **go light on raises**: heavy swinging turns a raise into a shrug. do: pick a weight you can control for 12 to 20 reps.
- #5 **add a cable raise**: the cable keeps tension at the bottom. do: one cable raise after your dumbbell sets.
