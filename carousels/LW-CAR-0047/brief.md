## Пункти
- #1 **abs**: the first thing people notice. they show when your body fat is low. do: hanging leg raise
- #2 **chest**: a full chest makes your whole torso look stronger. do: incline smith press
- #3 **shoulders**: round shoulders frame everything else. do: lateral raise
- #4 **back**: the v-shape people see from behind and from the front. do: pull-ups
- #5 **obliques**: sharper lines at the sides of your abs. do: cable woodchop
- #6 **legs**: a big top half on small legs is easy to spot at the beach. do: leg press
