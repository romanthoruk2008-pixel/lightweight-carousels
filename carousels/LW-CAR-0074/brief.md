## Пункти
- #1 **the hype man**: screams ‘one more’ when you’ve already done three more.
- #2 **the one who always cancels**: ‘can’t today, bro.’ every single time.
- #3 **the coach**: has a tip for every rep you do. asked or not.
- #4 **the talker**: one set, twenty minutes of conversation.
- #5 **the competitor**: whatever you lift, they add a plate.
- #6 **the silent one**: headphones in, no words, best workout of your week.
- #7 **the perfect one**: shows up on time, spots you, re-racks, goes home.
