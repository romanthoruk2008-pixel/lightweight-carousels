# LW-CAR-0034 — «you’re not a beginner anymore if…» (S2)

- 9 слайдів. На кожному велика галочка з пунктом, у кутку лічильник 1/7…7/7.
- **Маркетинг:** людина рахує свої «галочки» й пише в коментарях «6/7». Підпис: «How many did you get? Be honest 👇». Пункт 7 «you track every workout» сам підводить до Harda.

- you have a favorite exercise: and very strong opinions about it.
- you warm up without being told: a few light sets before the heavy ones, every time.
- you plan before you walk in: you know every exercise before you touch a weight.
- you feel the right muscle working: not just moving the weight from a to b.
- you stopped ego lifting: lighter weight, cleaner reps, better results.
- you rest on purpose: you know how long you need between sets.
- you track every workout: you know exactly what to beat next time.

**Фінал:** «harda tracks every set you lift, so you always know what to beat next time.»
