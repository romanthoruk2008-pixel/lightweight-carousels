## Маркетингова логіка
- **Корисно й знайомо всім:** у годину пік зайнято все. Кожен слайд дає заміну з рядком «do this:».
- **Коментарі:** «Which machine is always taken at your gym? 👇».

## Пункти
- #1 **bench press taken?**: same chest, no rack needed. do this: dumbbell press on any flat bench.
- #2 **squat rack taken?**: your quads don’t care which machine. do this: leg press or goblet squats.
- #3 **lat pulldown taken?**: same lats, same pull. do this: pull-ups or a single-arm cable pulldown.
- #4 **cable machine taken?**: dumbbells can copy most cable moves. do this: dumbbell raises, flys and curls.
- #5 **leg curl taken?**: your hamstrings still get the work. do this: romanian deadlifts with dumbbells.
- #6 **pec deck taken?**: a fly is a fly. do this: dumbbell flys on a flat bench.
- #7 **every machine taken?**: the gym is packed. you still came. do this: dumbbells, a bench and a plan.
