# LW-CAR-0036 — «secretly killing your gains» (S2)

- Нова версія «5 mistakes» (0012) у білому стилі, але з новими пунктами: не повторює ні 0012, ні 0029. 9 слайдів, макет 0029.
- **Маркетинг:** «secretly» інтригує, бо це звички, про які людина не думає (кардіо перед залом, телефон між підходами, вихідні). Підпис: «Be honest, how many are you doing? 👇».

- #1 cardio right before lifting: you start your heavy sets already tired. **fix:** lift first. do cardio after, or on another day.
- #2 rushing every rep: fast reps let momentum do the work. **fix:** control the weight on the way down.
- #3 endless phone breaks: rest turns into ten minutes and the workout falls apart. **fix:** set a rest timer and stick to it.
- #4 skipping your warm-up: cold muscles mean weaker first sets. **fix:** do a few light sets before the heavy ones.
- #5 copying someone else’s plan: their plan fits their body, their schedule and their level. **fix:** pick a plan that fits your week.
- #6 big nights out every weekend: a few heavy nights can undo a week of training. **fix:** keep drinking to the occasional one.
- #7 training 7 days a week: no rest days means no time to recover. **fix:** take at least one or two rest days a week.

**Фінал:** «harda tracks every set you lift, so you can see what’s actually working.»
