## Пункти
- #1 **slow down the way down**: same weight, way harder. no extra plates needed. do: take two seconds to lower every rep.
- #2 **film one set from the side**: your form looks different from how it feels. do: check your depth and your back on video.
- #3 **write down every set**: you can’t beat last week if you don’t remember it. do: log the weight and reps after each set.
- #4 **add a rep before you add weight**: small wins every week turn into big ones. do: hit the top of your rep range, then go heavier.
- #5 **rest longer on big lifts**: rushing makes your next set weaker. do: rest two to three minutes on squats and presses.
- #6 **train your weakest muscle first**: you have the most energy at the start. do: put the muscle you skip at the top of your workout.
- #7 **leave one rep in the tank**: most sets don’t need to go to failure to work. do: stop when the next rep would break your form.
