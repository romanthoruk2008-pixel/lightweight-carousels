## Пункти
- #1 **squat deep**: deeper reps train more of your legs. do: go as deep as you can with a flat back.
- #2 **train both hamstring jobs**: they bend the knee and hinge the hip. do: one curl and one hinge every week.
- #3 **add single-leg work**: it fixes a weaker side. do: bulgarian split squats or lunges.
- #4 **finish with leg extensions**: hit your quads hard without loading your back. do: two or three sets at the end.
- #5 **train legs twice a week**: two leg days beat one rushed one. do: a squat day and a hinge day.
