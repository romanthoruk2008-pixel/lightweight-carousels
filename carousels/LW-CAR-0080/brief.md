## Пункти
- #1 **side delts**: they set the top of the v. do: lateral raises in every upper workout.
- #2 **lats**: they spread your back out under your arms. do: lat pulldowns or pull-ups every week.
- #3 **upper back**: a thick upper back makes your whole frame look bigger. do: rows and face pulls.
- #4 **upper chest**: fills out the top of your t-shirt from the front. do: start chest day with an incline press.
- #5 **the waist**: the v only shows when your waist stays small. do: keep your body fat in check.
