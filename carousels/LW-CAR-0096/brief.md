## Пункти
- #1 **use a low incline**: a steep bench turns it into a shoulder press. do: set the bench low, not steep.
- #2 **press it first**: you are strongest at the start. do: start chest day with an incline press.
- #3 **fly from low to high**: the upper chest lifts your arms up and in. do: bring the handles up to chin height.
- #4 **tuck your elbows a little**: flared elbows load your shoulders. do: keep your elbows at about 45 degrees.
- #5 **add weight on a smith machine**: a fixed path makes progress easy to track. do: add a rep or a little weight every week.
