## Маркетингова логіка
- **Відкриття:** люди бачать вправи, яких не знали, і зберігають, щоб спробувати.
- **Без залу:** 4 з 7 вправ (deficit push-ups, reverse nordic, tibialis raise, copenhagen plank) робляться вдома.
- **Коментарі:** «я це роблю роками» / «а ще є X».

## Вправи
- hamstrings: **seated leg curl** — the stretch at your hip makes it a better curl than the lying one.
- side delts: **cable lateral raise** — tension right at the bottom, where dumbbells give you nothing.
- chest: **deficit push-ups** — a deeper stretch than normal push-ups, and no gym needed.
- quads: **reverse nordic** — loads your quads in a big stretch with just your bodyweight.
- grip & traps: **farmer’s carry** — grip, traps and core in one walk.
- shins: **tibialis raise** — the front of your shins. almost nobody trains them.
- inner thighs: **copenhagen plank** — your inner thighs and core, no equipment at all.
