## Пункти
- #1 **start with an incline press**: the upper chest fills out your t-shirt. do: incline dumbbell press first on chest day.
- #2 **get a deep stretch**: half reps cheat your chest. do: lower the weight until you feel a stretch.
- #3 **finish with a fly**: presses tire your triceps before your chest. do: pec deck or cable fly at the end.
- #4 **add one rep at a time**: more reps or more weight every week is how you grow. do: log every set and beat it next time.
- #5 **don’t forget dips**: a big stretch for your lower chest. do: lean forward and go deep.
