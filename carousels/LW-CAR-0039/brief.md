## Маркетингова логіка
- **Корисно новачкам і смішно бувалим:** кожен згадає, хто в його залі порушує яке правило.
- **Коментарі:** «Which rule do people break most at your gym? 👇».

## Пункти
- rule #1 **re-rack your weights**: if you can lift it, you can put it back.
- rule #2 **wipe down the bench**: nobody wants to lie in your sweat.
- rule #3 **give people space**: don’t stand right behind someone mid-set.
- rule #4 **ask how many sets are left**: then wait or work in. never just hover.
- rule #5 **don’t camp on a machine**: rest somewhere else so others can use it.
- rule #6 **no advice nobody asked for**: unless someone is about to get hurt.
- rule #7 **don’t film other people**: film yourself, not strangers.
