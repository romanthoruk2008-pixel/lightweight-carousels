## Пункти
- #1 **own the bottom stretch**: the stretch is the hardest part of the rep. do: hold the bottom for one second.
- #2 **use the full range**: from a deep stretch all the way to tiptoe. do: go all the way down and all the way up.
- #3 **train them straight and bent**: standing and seated raises hit them differently. do: one standing and one seated raise each week.
- #4 **train them often**: they carry you all day, so they recover fast. do: calves two or three times a week.
- #5 **mix heavy and light**: heavy sets and long burns both work. do: one heavy set, then one long set.
