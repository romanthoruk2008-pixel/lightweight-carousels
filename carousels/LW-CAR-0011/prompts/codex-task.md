Our own carousel, in the same series style. **The whole point is honesty.** Competitor transformation posts get called "unrealistic" in the comments. Ours shows a believable natural year.

## The most important rule: one figure, four honest stages
The same anatomical mannequin appears at four stages: **DAY 1, MONTH 3, MONTH 6, YEAR 1**. Same pose, same front view, same framing, same size on every slide where it appears, so swiping feels like watching one body change.
- **DAY 1:** an average untrained build. Narrow shoulders, flat chest, thin arms, a little soft around the waist, no visible definition.
- **MONTH 3:** almost the same, very slightly fuller shoulders and chest. The difference must be small.
- **MONTH 6:** clearly more muscular shoulders, chest and arms, a flatter waist, better posture. Natural.
- **YEAR 1:** an athletic, fit, natural physique: someone who obviously trains. **Not** a bodybuilder, not huge, not shredded.

Generate the four stages once and reuse exactly the same four figures on slides 1 and 3–6. Same mannequin style as the series: bald, faceless, **smooth plastic anatomy mannequin with a featureless pelvis**, plain short shorts in sand #D9CBBE, fine grey linework, sand #D9CBBE muscles, ivory skin. No coral highlight on these figures except where a slide says so.
