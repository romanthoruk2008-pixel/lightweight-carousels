## Пункти
- #1 **lose the layer on top**: everyone has abs. most are just covered. do: eat in a small calorie deficit.
- #2 **train abs like a muscle**: add weight, not just reps. do: weighted cable crunches.
- #3 **curl your hips up**: swinging your legs trains your hip flexors. do: hanging leg raises or reverse crunches.
- #4 **don’t skip obliques**: they frame your abs from the sides. do: cable woodchops or side planks.
- #5 **be patient**: abs show last when you lean out. do: log your workouts and stay consistent.
