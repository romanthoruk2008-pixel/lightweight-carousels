## Пункти
- #1 **an empty squat rack at 6pm**: you look around. nobody’s coming. it’s yours.
- #2 **the whole gym to yourself**: just you, your music and every machine.
- #3 **brand new plates**: no scratches, no rust. you almost don’t want to use them.
- #4 **the perfect pump**: your arms look twice as big. for about twenty minutes.
- #5 **grinding out the last rep**: slow, shaking, and it still went up.
- #6 **adding a plate you couldn’t lift last month**: same bar, more weight. that’s progress.
- #7 **the dumbbells you need, right where they belong**: in order, in pairs, on the rack. a miracle.
