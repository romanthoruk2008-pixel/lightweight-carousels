## Пункти
- #1 **ego lifting**: the weight goes up, the form goes home.
- #2 **no warm-up**: straight to the heaviest set, every single time.
- #3 **machine hostage**: ten minutes on the machine, zero sets, full scroll.
- #4 **never leg day**: big upper body, every week, no legs in sight.
- #5 **no plan at all**: a random workout every day, just vibes.
- #6 **training through sharp pain**: pushing through pain that isn’t normal.
- #7 **no idea what they lifted**: ask about last week and you get a shrug.
