## C. Marketing analysis

**Hook type.** A "definitive/ultimate list" authority hook ("BEST EXERCISES FOR EVERY MUSCLE GROUP") with a series qualifier "(Push Day Edition)". It works for four reasons:
- It promises a complete, optimized answer, which removes decision fatigue.
- The word "best" invites agreement or argument.
- "Push Day Edition" signals a series (pull/legs next), which encourages follows and saves.
- The cover shows the visual system right away (green-highlighted muscle on gym equipment), so viewers understand the format at a glance.

**Formula.** "Best exercises for every muscle in [split day]". There are 6 numbered muscle-region slides, each showing one target muscle and **2 interchangeable options ("A OR B")**, followed by a bonus-tip slide that is the app plug. Total: 1 hook + 6 items + 1 plug = 8 slides. Each item names a muscle sub-region (upper vs mid/low chest, front vs side delt, lateral vs long triceps head). That precision makes the post feel expert and invites debate.

**Practical value / saveability.** High and immediately usable: 12 named exercises that cover a full push day. The "OR" pairs give flexibility for different equipment. 40.5K saves against 73.9K likes confirms its value as a reference. Depth is minimal: no sets, reps, cues or why-explanations. The value is the selection plus the muscle mapping.

**Retention mechanics.**
- Numbered items (#1-#6) create a progress/completion pull.
- The same template on every slide makes swiping fast and the post feel easy to consume.
- The visual pattern (green muscle, arrows, two options) is satisfying and quick to scan.
- Muscles go from chest to shoulders to triceps, in the order of a real push workout.
- "Bonus Tip" on the last slide rewards finishing the carousel and hides the ad inside a "tip".
- Text is minimal, so each slide reads in about 1 second.

**CTA.** Implicit and soft, on the last slide only: "Track your workouts in every session / I use Arrow for that." It is phrased as a first-person recommendation (UGC voice) even though the account is the brand's own. There is no "download" or "link in bio" and no explicit follow/save ask on the slides or in the caption. The caption's "Find out what the best exercises are…" is an engagement line, not a CTA.

**Brand/app integration.** Soft sell. The brand appears only on slide 8 (the name "Arrow" in plain text plus a phone mockup). App features visible in the mockup:
- live workout logging with a session timer
- per-set kg x reps entry
- set repeat/copy icon
- unilateral toggle
- drop sets
- add/remove sets
- a muscle-map visualization of the muscles worked (which matches the carousel's green-highlighted-muscle visual language)
- social "Friends" tab, "Stats" tab, "Shop" tab

The carousel's illustration style (green muscle highlighting) echoes the app's muscle map, which ties content and product together visually.

**Fitness claims to verify (flag; do not reproduce as fact without sources):**
1. **"Triceps (Lateral Head)": JM Press / Machine Dips.** Head-specific claims are overstated. Pressing and dip patterns train all three heads. The lateral/medial heads may be relatively emphasized when the shoulder is not flexed, but evidence for isolating the lateral head is weak.
2. **"Triceps (Long Head)": "Triceps Extension" (apparently a cable pushdown variant).** A pushdown with the arm at the side is not typically classed as long-head-biased. Overhead extension, with the long head at a long muscle length, has support for greater long-head growth (e.g. Maeo et al., 2023, overhead vs pushdown). The left option on this slide is questionable, and the label "Triceps Extension" is vague. Commenters are visibly confused about this.
3. **"Mid/Low Chest": Flat Machine Press / Pec Dec.** These are reasonable for the sternal pecs. "Low chest" is usually linked to decline press/dips, so it is a mild oversimplification.
4. **"Upper Chest": Incline Press / Low-to-High Fly.** Broadly supported (clavicular head emphasis with shoulder flexion/incline), but "best" is subjective.
5. **"Best" in general** is an opinion, not a measured claim. The hook "every muscle group" covers only chest, front/side delts and triceps, leaving out rear delts and other muscles.
6. Front delt: DB / machine shoulder press, and side delt: DB / cable lateral raise. These are uncontroversial.

**Weaknesses / pushback.**
- Commenters question the triceps head mapping and ask for alternatives to equipment-specific moves (JM Press, machine dips).
- Some exercises are machine-dependent, which is a problem for home or limited-gym users.
- No explanation of why each exercise works.
- The app mockup shows "Pull Day" on a push-day post (small inconsistency).
- "I use Arrow for that" from the brand's own account reads as a disguised ad if viewers notice.
- "Every muscle group" overpromises for a push-only post.
